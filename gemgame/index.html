Cozy Match 3 Puzzle · iPhone

Match gems. Chain combos. Grow your garden.

GemGame is a cozy, handcrafted match 3 puzzle game for iPhone wrapped in a striking Galactic Casino look. Swap six gem types plus striped, wrapped, and rainbow specials into huge cascade combos, then spend the stars you earn restoring a starlit celestial garden. There are no ads, no forced timers, and no energy waits — a relaxing, offline matching game you play on your own terms.

What you get

A handcrafted match 3 puzzle with real depth: hand-designed levels on a fair, calibrated difficulty curve, deep special-gem chaining, and a garden meta-layer that turns the stars you collect into something you can see.

Cascade combos & special gems

Hand-designed match 3 levels with score, collect, and clear goals and a 1–3 star rating. Six gem types plus striped, wrapped, and rainbow special gems combine into huge cascade combos for genuine puzzle depth and big, satisfying payoffs.

Restore a starlit garden

A starlit garden meta-layer turns matching into progress you can see. Spend the stars you earn to rebuild celestial districts like the Starlight Promenade and Moonpetal Square, unlocking perks that boost your next puzzle run.

Daily rewards, Celestial Pass & missions

Come back for daily rewards, work through mission contracts, and climb a Celestial Pass reward track with both free and premium ladders. A lives system that refills over time, a coin economy, and a booster shop round out the loop.

Boosters & on-demand hints

Stuck on a tricky board? Tap an on-demand hint power-up, or play a Hammer, Shuffle, or Extra Moves booster when you want a little help — never because a timer is pressuring you to buy one.

Game Center leaderboards

For players who want real competition, GemGame includes three Game Center leaderboards (total stars, highest level, and best score) and six achievements. Skill decides the top, not your wallet.

No ads, no energy, offline

Relaxing, offline play with no ads, no third-party trackers, no forced timers, and no energy waits. Lives refill over time and restarts are fast, so you decide when a cozy session ends.
