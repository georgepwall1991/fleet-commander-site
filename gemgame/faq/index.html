Frequently Asked Questions

GemGame FAQ

Quick answers to the questions players ask most about GemGame, a cozy, relaxing match 3 puzzle game for iPhone with handcrafted levels, a starlit garden to restore, no ads, and no pay-to-win.

Get Support

Getting started and pricing

Is GemGame free?

Yes. GemGame is free to download and play on iPhone. There are optional in-app purchases such as coin packs, per-chapter Celestial Passes, and a one-time Hard Mode Pack, but no purchase is required to play.

Does GemGame have ads?

No. GemGame has no ads and no third-party trackers. You will never be interrupted by video ads or banners while you play.

Can I play GemGame offline without internet or wifi?

Yes. GemGame is designed for relaxing offline play, so you can match gems on a plane, a commute, or anywhere with no wifi. An internet connection is only needed for optional extras like in-app purchases and Game Center leaderboards.

Does GemGame have an energy system or timers I have to wait on?

GemGame has a friendly lives system, which is a light form of energy: starting a level spends one life, and lives refill over time for free, or you can refill instantly with an optional purchase. There is no countdown clock during a level, though, because levels are scored by moves rather than time, so you can take as long as you like on each move.

Gameplay and features

What are striped, wrapped, and rainbow gems?

These are special gems you create by matching more than three at once. Striped gems clear a whole row or column, wrapped gems explode in a burst, and rainbow gems clear every gem of one color. Combining specials chains into huge cascade combos.

What is the garden meta-layer in GemGame?

Beyond the match 3 levels, GemGame has a starlit garden you restore over time. You spend the stars you earn from puzzles to rebuild celestial districts like the Starlight Promenade and Moonpetal Square, unlocking perks that boost your puzzle runs.

What is the Celestial Pass and the Hard Mode Pack?

The Celestial Pass is a reward track with both a free ladder and an optional premium ladder of bonus rewards, sold per chapter. The Hard Mode Pack is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks tougher levels for players who want a bigger challenge. Both are optional.

Does GemGame have Game Center leaderboards and achievements?

Yes. GemGame supports Apple Game Center with three leaderboards, for total stars, highest level, and best score, plus six achievements to earn as you progress.

Progress, devices, and purchases

Is my progress saved, and does it sync across devices?

Your progress is saved on your device, so your levels, stars, coins, and garden are kept between sessions. There is no separate cloud account, so progress stays on the iPhone where you play.

Is GemGame on Android or iPad?

GemGame is an iPhone game on iOS. There is no Android version, and it is built and tested for iPhone.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-game shop and use the restore button, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Non-consumable purchases like the Hard Mode Pack will be re-applied to your account.
