Celestial Pass

The Celestial Pass: a free and optional premium reward track

The Celestial Pass is GemGame's reward ladder. As you complete match-3 levels you earn experience that lifts you up its tiers, handing out bonus coins, boosters, and decorations along the way. There is a free ladder everyone climbs, and an optional premium ladder that adds extra rewards for the players who want them.

How the pass works

The pass is organised per chapter rather than on a ticking real-world clock, so you climb it by playing, not by racing a countdown. Every level you finish contributes experience toward the next tier. Reach a tier and you unlock its reward immediately.

  • Free ladder: open to every player, with useful rewards at each tier.
  • Premium ladder: an optional purchase that adds a parallel set of bonus rewards on the same tiers.
  • XP from play: you progress simply by completing levels — no separate grind.

Cozy, not pressured

Because the Celestial Pass is tied to your chapter progress instead of a timed season, you are never punished for taking a break. There is no FOMO clock counting down your rewards. You play at your own pace, the pass fills as you go, and the premium ladder stays entirely optional — GemGame is free to download and fully playable without it.
