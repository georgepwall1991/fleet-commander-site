No ads, no energy

A match 3 with no ads, no energy, and full offline play

If you have ever closed a match-3 game because of a forced video ad or an energy bar that made you wait, GemGame is built for you. It is a cozy, handcrafted match-3 puzzle for iPhone with no ads, no energy meter gating your moves, and full offline play — free to download.

No ads, at all

GemGame contains no third-party advertising. There are no banners, no interstitials, and no rewarded video ads interrupting your run. It also ships with no third-party ad or tracking SDKs, so your match-3 sessions stay clean and uninterrupted from the first tap.

No energy pressure on your moves

There are no forced timers and no countdown clock pressuring your moves during a level — you can think as long as you like. GemGame does use a friendly lives system that refills over time, but it never rushes a move you are making, and every level is solvable at your own pace.

Plays offline, free to download

GemGame is designed for relaxing offline play, so you can match gems on a plane, on a commute, or anywhere with no wifi. An internet connection is only needed for optional extras such as in-app purchases and Game Center leaderboards. It is free to download, with only optional in-app purchases — making it the relaxing, ad-free, no-energy match-3 many players are looking for.
