No ads, no pay-to-win

A match 3 with no ads, no pay-to-win, and full offline play

If you have ever closed a match-3 game because of a forced video ad or a pay-to-win difficulty wall built to sell you boosters, GemGame is built for you. It is a cozy, handcrafted match-3 puzzle for iPhone with no ads, no pay-to-win spikes, and full offline play — free to download, with lives that refill over time for free.

No ads, at all

GemGame contains no third-party advertising. There are no banners, no interstitials, and no rewarded video ads interrupting your run. It also ships with no third-party ad or tracking SDKs, so your match-3 sessions stay clean and uninterrupted from the first tap.

No countdown clock on your moves

There is no countdown clock pressuring your moves during a level — you can think as long as you like, because levels are scored by moves, not time. GemGame does use a friendly lives system that refills over time for free, but it never rushes a move you are making, and every level is solvable at your own pace.

Plays offline, free to download

GemGame is designed for relaxing offline play, so you can match gems on a plane, on a commute, or anywhere with no wifi. An internet connection is only needed for optional extras such as in-app purchases and Game Center leaderboards. It is free to download, with only optional in-app purchases — making it the relaxing, ad-free match-3 many players are looking for.
