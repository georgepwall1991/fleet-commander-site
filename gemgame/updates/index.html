Garden polish & reward flow

v1.0.1 Released 28 May 2026

The first update after launch focuses on the celestial garden hub and on making sure every finished level reliably shows its result, so progressing from one level to the next stays smooth.

  • Rebuilt the Garden hub with clearer navigation and richer landmark art.
  • Added safeguards so completed runs always show the win or retry result.
  • Fixed a reward-claim edge case where tapping Continue could stay on the completed level.
  • Tightened gameplay result handling for smoother level-to-level progression.

Initial App Store release

v1.0 Launch

The version that started it all: GemGame arrived on the App Store as a complete, cozy match 3 puzzle game with a starlit garden to restore and plenty of live-ops to keep coming back to. The whole feature set landed in this first release.

  • Handcrafted match 3 levels with score, collect, and clear goals and a 1-3 star rating.
  • The garden restoration meta-layer, where you spend stars to rebuild celestial districts.
  • Daily rewards to claim each time you return.
  • The Celestial Pass reward track with free and premium ladders.
  • Mission contracts for extra goals and rewards.
  • The booster shop, stocking the Hint, Hammer, Shuffle, and Extra Moves power-ups.
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements.
