Features

  • Single-player space strategy with connected star lanes and map control
  • Fleet combat built around staging, attack timing, and reinforcement routes
  • Galactic expansion that forces real frontier choices instead of free warps
  • Offline progress that keeps your economy and production moving
  • Optional Game Center achievements and weekly rankings for return goals

Why It Sticks

The early game is built around fast clarity: claim the second system, field a real strike fleet, and learn the first frontier defense before the map gets noisy. Later runs layer in research routing, border recovery, optional social goals, and long-run reset timing without turning the core conquest loop into a menu grind.

Optional Research Lab subscriptions remain a support layer, not the main reason to discover or play the game.
