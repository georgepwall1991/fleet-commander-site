Opening Arc And Command Center Growth Pass

17 March 2026

This pass is aimed at two things at once: getting new commanders to their first clean win faster, and making the public-facing App Store and website surfaces sharper for organic discovery.

First-session clarity

The onboarding and opening objective flow now concentrate on the first colony, the first fleet, and the first border battle instead of front-loading long-run reset systems. The goal is a cleaner ten-to-fifteen-minute path to understanding the actual conquest loop.

Command Center social layer

Fleet Commander now has a dedicated Command Center for optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements. Weekly rankings and visible achievement progress create return goals without adding a backend or interrupting single-player flow.

Growth surface refresh

The App Store copy and website now lead more directly with connected-star-lane conquest, fleet command, and frontier pressure. Support, privacy, and terms stay available as compliance pages, while the homepage and update pages carry the discoverable product story.

What comes next

The next growth steps are App Store screenshot headline experiments, custom product pages for strategy and offline/prestige audiences, and a weekly report loop driven by App Store Connect analytics plus review summarization.
