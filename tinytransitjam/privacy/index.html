Privacy Policy

Tiny Transit Jam

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Tiny Transit Jam is an iPhone puzzle game. The short version: the developer does not collect your data. The game has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves its state locally on your iPhone:

  • Level progress, stars and best results
  • Coins and booster inventory
  • Daily puzzle results and streak
  • Settings such as sound, haptics and colour-blind palette
  • Which purchases you have made, as reported by Apple

This data is not sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes it.

Apple services

The game uses Apple services that Apple runs under its own privacy policy:

  • App Store in-app purchases. Apple processes payments; the developer never sees your payment details.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in, your scores and achievements are shared with Game Center so leaderboards work.

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Advertising and tracking

Tiny Transit Jam contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Email the developer.
