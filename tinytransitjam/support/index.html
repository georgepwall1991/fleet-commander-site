Tiny Transit Jam Support

Thanks for playing. If something isn't working, the fixes below cover the common cases. If they don't help, email me and I'll reply as soon as I can.

Email support

Common fixes

  • A purchase didn't arrive: open the Store and tap Restore Purchases. Coin packs are added to your balance right away; if one is missing, email me with the date of purchase.
  • Sound or haptics: open Settings and switch Sound Effects or Haptics on or off.
  • Progress looks out of date: close the app completely and reopen it.
  • Game Center isn't signed in: sign in under iPhone Settings › Game Center, then reopen the game.

When you get in touch

It helps to include:

  • The app version (shown in Settings) and your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The level number, or the Daily puzzle date
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Please don't send payment details or passwords.
