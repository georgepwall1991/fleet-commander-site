Fleet Commander

A solo tactical space roguelite about fleet builds, dangerous routes, rival commanders, and knowing when to extract.

Facts

  • Developer: George Wall
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad, iOS/iPadOS17 or later
  • Model: free download with optional in-app purchases
  • Frontier Runs: upcoming version1.2.0; not yet publicly released

The game

Choose a route through a hostile sector. Compare tactical forecasts, commit to Blitz, Flank, or Fortify, then adapt your fleet with modules and protocols. Face multi-stage rival encounters, bank salvage, and carry reserves and fleet history into the next run.
